Auto Refresh — Page Monitor & Reload

Set it. Forget it. Never hit reload again.

1. Open any page you want to keep fresh — a dashboard, live scores, an auction.

2. Click the Auto Refresh icon, pick an interval, hit Start. A live countdown begins instantly.

3. Want an alert instead? Choose “Monitor for changes”, pick an element, and close the tab — we’ll still watch it.
